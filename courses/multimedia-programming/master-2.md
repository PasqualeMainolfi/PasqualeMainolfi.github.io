# Multimedia Programming: Master 2

**Focus:** Reading the brain: brain-computer interfaces (BCI) as a control and expressive medium.

**Path:** **Bachelor 1** create → **Bachelor 2** connect → **Bachelor 3** perceive → **Master 1** sense the body → **Master 2 (this course)** read the brain

| | |
|:--|:--|
| **Duration** | 30h |
| **Study hours** | 120h |
| **CFA** | 6 |
| **Prerequisites** | Multimedia Programming, Master 1 |
| **Evaluation** | Exam |
| **Notes** | Given the need to work with sensors, soldering, and other related hardware aspects, this course will have a close collaboration with the Electroacoustics course COME/04. The collaboration will provide students with the opportunity to integrate sound and audio elements into their multimedia projects, thereby enriching the overall experience and enhancing the interdisciplinarity of the course. Joint sessions or workshops will be conducted, allowing students from both courses to collaborate, share knowledge, and explore the integration of sensors, audio components, and other electroacoustic elements in their multimedia works. |

## Learning outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to:

- describe the types of BCI and their neuroscientific basis
- acquire and pre-process neural signals
- analyse neural signals and extract features for classification
- design and build an interactive work controlled by a BCI

## Units

### 1. BCI and neuroscience fundamentals (6h)

- BCI interfaces and their role in multimedia design
- Current applications of BCI interfaces
- Invasive, non-invasive and hybrid BCI
- Fundamentals of neuroscience and neural signal processing
- **Lab:** first recordings with an EEG headset

### 2. Acquisition and pre-processing (8h)

- Techniques for neural signal acquisition and recording
- Pre-processing and filtering of neural signals
- **Lab:** recording and cleaning EEG data (Emotiv EPOC+ / EPOC X)

### 3. Analysis and classification (8h)

- Spectral and temporal analysis of neural signals
- Feature extraction and classification techniques
- **Lab:** detecting events from neural signals

### 4. BCI in art and multimedia (4h)

- Applications of BCI in multimedia and music
- Latest research and developments
- **Lab:** choosing the interaction modes and events to control

### 5. A BCI-driven work (4h)

- Actions and responses based on events detected by the BCI
- **Lab:** design and development of a simple interactive work based on BCI

## Assessment

**Exam.** A project on the course topics, agreed with the instructor in advance. Rules and criteria: see [Assessment](index.md#assessment) on the course page.
