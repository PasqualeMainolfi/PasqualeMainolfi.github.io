# FAQ

**What study material is recommended for the course?**
: Open your course from the [Courses](courses/index.md) page. Each course page lists objectives, references and required software; each program page lists the topics and suggested readings for your year.

**How can I contact the instructor?**
: If this page does not answer your question, write to <pasquale.mainolfi@consalerno.it> or on Telegram ([@PakoMaino](https://t.me/PakoMaino)).

**What attendance is required?**
: At least 70% of the total hours scheduled for your course.

**How will I be evaluated at the end of the course?**
: It depends on your study plan: check whether the course is marked as *exam* or *eligibility*. The evaluation details are in the program page of your course.

**When are the exam sessions?**
: Unless there are exceptional circumstances, exam sessions are held during the last week of each month. I will let you know the date informally; the official communication comes from the administration. Booking the exam through the online service is mandatory.

**What if I miss a lecture or an exam for a justified reason?**
: Missed hours cannot be made up, even when the absence is justified. You can miss up to 30% of the total hours of your course without losing the right to be evaluated.

**Where do I find lecture notes, code and assignments?**
: In the [Lecture Notes](lecture-notes/index.md), [Scripts](https://github.com/PasqualeMainolfi/PasqualeMainolfi.github.io/tree/main/scripts) and [Assignments](assignments/index.md) sections.

**Are extraordinary exam sessions possible?**
: Only when strictly necessary. Contact me by email.

**How do I ask for a thesis with me as supervisor?**
: Read the [Thesis](thesis.md) page first: it covers procedures, selection criteria and formatting rules.

**How do you select thesis students?**
: The proposed work must be closely related to my field and reflect the concepts and skills covered in my courses. I favour experimental work that explores new ideas, methods and approaches. And time: a thesis needs time, so get in touch early.
